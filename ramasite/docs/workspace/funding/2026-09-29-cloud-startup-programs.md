## Scaleway Startup Program — https://www.scaleway.com/en/startup-program/

Eligible: < 5 years, < 50 employees, not yet a Scaleway client. Entry is the **Founders Program (€1,000, 12
months)**; Early Stage (€9,000) needs ~€500/month spend, Growth (€36,000) ~€1,500/month. Identity verification
is mandatory before review.

- **Product (short):** Swypik is a video-native commerce platform with a multi-tenant ERP for small European
  businesses; SwypikOS is a local-first operating system whose AI agent (Ilaria, our own model) runs on the
  user's device and acts only with the user's approval.
- **What we will build on Scaleway:** training and evaluating Ilaria language models with native 1.58-bit
  (ternary) weights on H100 GPU instances, and hosting Swypik's EU-only inference/API backend. Sovereign EU
  hosting matters to us because Swypik handles European merchants' data under GDPR.
- **Traction / stage:** product built (commerce, ERP, video feed, seller/creator/host portals), pre-launch;
  ternary training validated on one GPU (125M: perplexity 27.0 ternary vs 28.2 bf16); open-source trainer
  https://github.com/office233/ilaria; applications in review at EuroHPC (Fast Lane, Large Scale, Benchmark,
  Development); accepted into Google for Startups Immersion EMEA.
- **Why Scaleway:** EU sovereignty, H100/L40S availability, predictable pricing for a bootstrapped startup.
